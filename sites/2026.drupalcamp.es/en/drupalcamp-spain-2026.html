About DrupalCamp

DrupalCamp Spain brings together both leading web development specialists and individuals who wish to deepen their knowledge or simply learn about the tool. The goal of DrupalCamp Spain is to promote the use of Drupal and serve as a meeting point for the Spanish community.

MADRID

Read more

This year, the event will take place in Madrid, presented as an accessible connection point for the rest of the country and an ideal setting to strengthen ties between companies, share ideas, and give visibility to the new initiatives shaping the future of Drupal.

The event will be held at Talent Garden, a leading space for innovation and entrepreneurship located in the heart of the capital. Its prime location, just minutes from Delicias, Méndez Álvaro, and Atocha stations, ensures easy access both for those coming from other parts of Madrid and for attendees traveling from the rest of Spain.

Both Madrid and Talent Garden offer an inspiring environment, designed to foster connections within the Drupal community and collaboration among industry professionals.

Mike Herchel

Lullabot
@mikeherchel
https://www.drupal.org/u/mherchel
https://github.com/mherchel

Mike Herchel has been creating websites since 2001, and developing with Drupal since 2007. He is one of the lead organisers of Florida Drupalcamp, and is always willing to advocate for the Drupal community and the web development community at-large. He’s a front-end developer at Lullabot and is a host of the Lullabot podcast, which is a bi-weekly podcast on everything Drupal, design, and web development. Mike actually enjoys solving cross-browser issues, and is passionate about the rapid pace of modern front-end development. He lives with his feisty 6 year old daughter, 11 year old golden retriever, and a mischievous chocolate lab puppy.

Mike's sessions will be Putting Yourself Out There and Avoiding the “What Ifs” and Debugging, Profiling, & Rocking Out with Browser-Based Developer Tools!.

Business Day

This DrupalCamp will host the 4th Business Day, focused on facilitating networking for CxOs and taking the pulse of the market.

What are we going to do?

Group dynamics: In this space we will encourage to meet each other, break the ice, and create an environment conducive to networking.
 
Business Panel: Who are you? What are you looking for? Prepare your business cards because you will have a panel to present your company.

We're going to play! Yes, we're going to play, share, and learn while we will enjoy the event. We will use a game to work together about different relevant topics in our company day to day: team management, communication, cooperation.
 
Open Space: Based on everyone's feedback, the group will divide in smaller ones to discuss specific topics.

Schedule

  09:00 - 09:10   Welcome
  09:10 - 09:30   Introductions - Business Panel
  09:30 - 10:00   Let's play!
  10:00 - 11:45   Open Space
  11:45 - 14:00   Open Space
  14:00 - 14:30   Retrospective
  14:30 - 15:30   Lunch

How can I participate?

Buy your Business Day ticket. If you are Gold or Silver sponsor it is already included. We will send you an email to know what do you expect from the Business Day.

If you proposed a session and it was selected, we will make sure that it does not collide with the Business Day.

When?

Friday the 5th of May starting at 09:00AM.

Where?

La Casa Encendida (Madrid), where DrupalCamp will take place.
 

More info

If you have any doubts, just send as an email at networking [at] drupal [dot] es.

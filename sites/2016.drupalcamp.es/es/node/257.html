About the event

This year the DrupalCamp Spain is including the following:

Sprints:

From Tuesday to Sunday, the Drupal Community has an appointment in Granada to contribute to Drupal.

Place: Sciences park

Business Day:

During the Friday journey, we are having a special appointment with all the CFO of Drupal to discuss about how to improve the use of Drupal and to talk about project management and Drupal business.

Place: Sciences park

Drupal 8 Training:

Forcontu is offering a free Drupal 8 training for beginners. The training is on Friday morning from 10h till 14h

Place: ETSIIT University of Granada,  Periodista Daniel Saucedo Aranda, s/n.

Sessions and BoFs:

As usual, on Saturday and Sunday we are having sessions and BoF in spanish and english covering all the possible topics and levels. Don't miss the group picture!

Place: Sciences park

Visit the Alhambra:

On Friday at 21h we are offering a special guide visit to the Alhambra. You can buy your ticket with yout event ticket. We only have 30 Alhambra tickets!

About the Sciences Park:

Is the first interactive science museum of Andalucia. It has several conference rooms, a very beautiful outside with gardens and a tower from you can view all the skyline of Granada

It is close to the downtown. You can go by foot in 15 minutes

It has public park with a low cost for visitors

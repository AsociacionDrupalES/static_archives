Venue

Las Cigarreras was Alicante's tobaco factory back in the 19th century. It was re-purposed five years ago as a cultural and innovation center.

The venue has everything that a DrupalCamp needs: an auditory, conference rooms, meeting areas for BoFs, a dinning area, plus a great location just a few minutes away from the city center.

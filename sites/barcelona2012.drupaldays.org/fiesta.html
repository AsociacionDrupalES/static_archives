Fiesta

On Friday and Saturday we will organize a hook_great_fiesta($beer, $wine) for all the Drupal developers that are in town. This will give you the chance to meet fellow-Drupalers and have a beer && wine || (water || coffee) with them and talk Barça, cars, politics, hooks and many of the other essential building blocks of life.

Friday 15th.

  • Time: 20:00 h. to 22:00 h.
  • Place: Bar del convent de Sant Agustí - More info
  • Location: C/Comerç 36, Barcelona
  • Metro: Jaume I
  • What: cold beers and fine wines

Saturday 16th.

  • Time: 20:30 h. to 23:00 h.
  • Place: Chiringito El Bierzo, Nova Icaria beach
  • Location: Lat 41.390815, Long 2.202248
  • Metro: Ciudadella - Villa Olimpica

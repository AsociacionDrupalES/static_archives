Call for Speakers

We are now accepting session submissions. If you want to present in Barcelona, you should start thinking now about a good subject that will capture the attention of a crowd of mainly programmers.

The conference is targeted at Drupal developers with varying skill sets: from the beginners to experts. The sessions should focus on technical topics.

Please have a look at the speaker's guidelines that we assembled.

If you have any questions, please use our forum, contact form or ping @drupaldaysbcn on Twitter.

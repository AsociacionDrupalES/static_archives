A Software Carol (Keynote)

Sala
Auditorio
Autor/a
angela_barriga
Categoría
New to Drupal
Audiencia
All Attendees
Recent AI advancements seem to bring a never-ending, frenetic rhythm of innovation and uncertainty, to the point where just keeping up can be exhausting. Amidst this raging storm, it is important to ask ourselves, as developers: Where do we come from? And more importantly, where do we (want to) go from here?

In this talk, we will explore the past, present, and future of software development. We will debunk myths and examine how AI can benefit us rather than bring us doom.

Categoría

Audiencia

Currently, Drupal Core still provides a legacy method for displaying autocomplete elements in a tag-style format. However, there is a wide variety of modules within the Drupal ecosystem that offer modern solutions to enhance these elements. Among them, the Tagify module not only modernizes and improves the user interface for autocomplete elements but also introduces exclusive features that no other module offers, significantly improving the overall user experience.

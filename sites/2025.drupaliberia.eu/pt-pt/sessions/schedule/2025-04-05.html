It's a bird! it's a plane! it's Symfony Live Components!

Sala
Auditorio
Autor/a
nuez
Categoría
Off the "Drupal Island"
Público
Intermediate
When building a UI in Drupal, you face a choice: decoupled or traditional Drupal? Decoupled Drupal offers powerful UI/UX but introduces complexity and budget overruns. Building in Drupal means dealing with Drupal's own Ajax Commands and other quirks. What if you could create a React-like experience without the complexity of going decoupled? Symfony Live Components does just that, allowing dynamic UIs with minimal setup. In this talk, I’ll showcase real-life examples from Mapsemble.com to demonstrate its power and simplicity.

Free Software has always been a crucial aspect of fostering an Open Web, of ensuring people's rights to expression, agency, and privacy, a fulcrum of collective sense-making and an enabler of online civics.

These factors have never been more important than today, not have they ever been under such a concerted attack as today.

We will examine our role as users and makers of Free, Open Source Software, and how our everyday actions transcend our jobs and hobbies, and form one of the backbones of a better tomorrow.

In this presentation, we will explore the pivotal role that associations play in fostering and promoting Drupal communities. We will delve into the various ways these associations support community growth, from providing resources and networking opportunities to organizing events that bring members together.
By highlighting the benefits of becoming a member or a sponsor company and actively participating in these associations, we aim to demonstrate how individual or organisation contributions can significantly enhance the collective strength and impact of the Drupal ecosystem.

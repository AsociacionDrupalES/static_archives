Frequently Asked Questions

h3. What is a DrupalCamp? What is Drupal?

Drupal is a free open-source content management system (CMS) written in the PHP programming language.

Drupal Camps are community sharing + training events for Drupal enthusiasts, designers, clients, and especially for newbies.

h3. How Much Does it Cost to Attend?

Very little! The first 50 registered pay 25€, and the rest pay 35€. This moderate fee goes toward paying the cost of food. Thanks to our amazing sponsors, the cost of offering a top class professional event at an excellent venue are covered.

h3. How Big is the Conference?

We don't know yet. We have 425 tentative attendees registered. The Citilab venue can accommodate 400 - 450 attendees, so we're hoping to fit everyone in without limiting the event registration.

h3. What is the main language of the Conference?

The main language will be Spanish, with English sessions from International speakers and participants. There will be 4 sessions tracks with at least one of them in English exclusively.

h3. Why do I need to create a profile on this site?

We use this information for your conference badge, along with some other information to make meeting like-minded people easier. You also need a profile in order to pick sessions.

h3. Why do I need to Pick Sessions?

We depend on this information to adjust the program as best as possible to the actual requirements, for smooth operation on the day.

h3. Can I register more than one person at once?

Unfortunately, no. Each person's profile holds their conference registration information.

h3. Will I receive any registration materials in the mail?

No, everything is handled on-line. We require your Registration ID to accredit you on arrival at DrupalCamp.

h3. Can I pay with a purchase order/check/cash?

We are only accepting PayPal at this time. Paypal does not require you to sign-up with them before paying and accepts all major credit cards. If for some reason you CANNOT pay via PayPal (International restrictions etc) please send an email to the camp organizer via our contact form.

h3. Is DrupalCamp good for newbies?

Absolutely!

The DrupalCamp event is aimed at beginners, experts, companies, clients and end users. There are 4 simultaneous sessions planned, which treat the many aspects of Drupal.

There is even an English language session branch for International Participants!

h3. Can I still become a sponsor of DrupalCamp Spain?

Yes! Check out the different packages here and contact us.

h3. Still have an unanswered question?

Please ask us in the Forum.

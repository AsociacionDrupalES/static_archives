Drupal is much more than just a piece of software that you download. Whether you are a newbie, expert, coder, designer, or an end user, we all have an effect on the ecosystem where the sum is greater than its parts. We'll take a look at the big picture of the Drupal world, how to leverage more than just code, and how you can have more impact than you think.

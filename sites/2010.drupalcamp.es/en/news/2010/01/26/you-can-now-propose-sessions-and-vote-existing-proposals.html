You can now propose sessions - and vote on existing proposals!

Tue, 26/01/2010 - 14:10

The submission period for sending in session proposals for the Drupalcamp is officially open. All registered users have the opportunity to put forward session proposals which will be democratically voted by the other assistants.

Go on - put yours forward!

You can also visit your favourite sessions on the Sessions Page. Sessions which receive the most votes are included in the final programme, so please vote!

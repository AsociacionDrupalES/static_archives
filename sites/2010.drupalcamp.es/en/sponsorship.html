Sponsorship

What is it?

DrupalCamp Barcelona 2010 will be the biggest drupal event in Spain since Drupalcon 2007 and will bring together more than 250 people from many areas; developers, designers, customers and companies, national and international people who share an in Drupal in Drupal Barcelona for 3 days.

When and where?

26, 27 and 28 of February 2010.
Citilab in Cornellà de Llobregat (Barcelona).

Why would you become a sponsor?

DrupalCamp is a non-commercial event and sponsorship is vital for our success. The event is organized and run entirely by volunteers, but there are obviously costs to be covered, such as the venue and publicity. Sponsoring the DrupalCamp Spain is an excellent opportunity to promote your business in an environment of innovative technologies such as the Drupal community and Open Source values, and also to give you a chance to connect with talent and clients who are attending the DrupalCamp.

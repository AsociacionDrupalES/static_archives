Accommodation

We have arranged special prices for DrupalCamp attendees in a few hotels. To benefit from these prices, you will need to make the reservation via phone or email stating that you are attending to DrupalCamp Spain 2015. Take into account that the hotel may ask your credit card details to confirm reservation.

If you want to take a look on the recommended hotels position and the most important places on DrupalCamp, see this map.

Hotel Los Jandalos Jerez 4*

This very central hotel is located minutes from Universitary Campus and a few meters from the place where the Saturday party will be held. If you want to see the location, take a look to this map.

Contact phone: (34) 956 32 72 30

Contact email: reservas.jerez@jandalos.com

Web: http://www.hoteljandalosjerez.com/

Price per night + buffet breakfast.

  • Double room for individual use: 65,00€ (VAT incluided)
  • Double room: 75,00€ (VAT incluided)

 

Hotel Tryp Jerez 4*

Located on the heart of the city, and 5 min from the most important wine factories on Jerez. This hotel has parking area. If you want to know the exact location, take a look at this map.

Contact phone: (34) 956 32 70 30

Contact email: tryp.jerez@melia.com

Web: http://www.tryphotels.com/es/hotel-tryp-jerez-espana.html

Price per night + buffet breakfast.

  • Double room for individual use: 75,00€ (VAT incluided)
  • Double room: 85,00€ (VAT incluided)

 

Hotel NH Avenida Jerez 4*

Enjoy a fantastic location from where you will be able to discover Jerez de la Frontera. This hotel is 2 minutes walk from the city center, and only 5 minutes from the Fundación Real Escuela del Arte Ecuestre. The Centro de Flamenco Andaluz and the bullfight place are also very close. If you want to see the hotel location, see this map.

Contact phone: (34) 956 34 74 11

Contact mail: nhavenidadejerez@nh-hotels.com

Web: https://www.nh-hoteles.es/hotel/nh-avenida-jerez

Price per night + buffet breakfast.

  • Double room for individual use: 60,00€ (VAT incluided)
  • Double room: 70,00€ (VAT incluided)

Hotel H2 Jerez 4*

Hotel H2 Jerez is integrated on Nuevo Chapín stadium, only 15 minutes walk from the city center. The building has the form of an ellipse, so the rooms are very unique and spacious, having 36 of them views to the stadium indoor itself. If you want to check the location, take a look to this map.

Contact phone: (34) 956 32 72 22

Contact mail: jerez@hoteles2.com

  • Double room for individual use: 49,00€ (VAT incluided)

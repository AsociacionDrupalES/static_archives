Sponsorship

Drupal Iberia brings together leading web development experts, as well as professionals and enthusiasts who want to deepen their knowledge or discover the potential of Drupal.

The main goal of Drupal Iberia is to promote the use of Drupal and act as a bridge between the Portuguese and Spanish communities, fostering collaboration, knowledge sharing, and the growth of the Drupal ecosystem across the Iberian Peninsula.

The event is organized on a rotating basis between the two countries:

  • 2024 – Évora (Portugal)
  • 2025 – Cáceres (Spain)
  • 2026 – Braga (Portugal)

Download the sponsorship deck

Learning and Knowledge Sharing

Drupal Iberia provides a collaborative environment where attendees can exchange practical experience, discover new tools and approaches, and stay up to date with the latest developments in the Drupal ecosystem through sessions, workshops, and informal discussions.

Promotion of Drupal and its Community

The event showcases Drupal as a robust open-source platform while highlighting the strength, diversity, and impact of its community across Portugal, Spain, and the broader European ecosystem.

Strengthening the Network of Collaborators, Companies, and Enthusiasts

Drupal Iberia fosters meaningful connections between developers, agencies, companies, and community members, encouraging long-term collaboration, partnerships, and the growth of a sustainable Drupal network in the Iberian region.

The cheap tickets at 25€ have gone!

Mon, 11/01/2010 - 17:36

The first 50 tickets at 25€ have gone in 2 days. What a success! The rest of the tickets are now at 35€, not a bad price at all for three days of sessions, plenty of food and lots of fun, is it?

Don't wait to the last minute! We had more that 425 pre-registrations, and we may have to close ticket sales if we reach maximum capacity of the Citilab venue (425-450 people).

Early registration also helps us to plan aspects of the event such as space allocation, catering and merchandising.

The early bird catches the worm - don't miss out!

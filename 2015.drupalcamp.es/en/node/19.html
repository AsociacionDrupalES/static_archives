Information

Drupalcamp Spain 2015 will take place in Jerez de la Frontera from 22 to 24 May 2015. The event will be organized into 2 groups:

Business day

This block of the event, one day long (May 22nd) will be the opening event. Group dynamics and talks focused on establishing communication between attendees and specialized firms will be performed. It will take place at the Palacio de Villavicencio, a renaissance building in the Alcazar's Monumental complex.

Developers days

Mainly day event, oriented to people who want to start or have knowledge about development in Drupal CMS. Will take place on Saturday 23rd and Sunday 24th May at the Campus of Jerez, home of Cadiz University in the city. There will be an introductory course to Drupal CMS and 3 meeting rooms with different level sessions, so we can share knowledge about our favorite content manager.

Do you find it interesting? This is only the beginning of what we have prepared! Come to Drupalcamp 2015!

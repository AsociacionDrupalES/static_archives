Bio: 
Peter is working with Drupal since version 4.7 when Drupal was barely known to the larger public. He learnt PHP with the elegant MVC framework CakePHP. In 2007, he worked on what was at that time one of the first templates for Drupal: ESN Satellite. Since its inception, the template website targeted at student sections from the Erasmus Student Network has revolutionized the web presence of the network. That success led him to set up the not-for-profit organization Youth Agora (http://www.youthagora.org) in 2008. Youth Agora participates in various exciting European Projects, building online youth platforms such as Sheryica or the international Masters Course Template within the Europan Campus project. In 2009, he co-founded Nuvole (http://www.nuvole.org), a Drupal web company specialized in international projects. In 2011, Peter completed his PhD in Bio-engineering Sciences at the Free University of Brussels, and is currently working as a post-doctoral researcher at the Barcelona Biomedical Research Park. He is also a part-time freelancer working with Drupal and maintaining several Drupal modules.

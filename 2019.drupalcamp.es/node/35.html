Business Day

This year, we will host the 6th Business Day, focused on facilitating networking for CxOs and taking the pulse of the market.

What are we going to do?

  • 10:00 - 10:30 Presentations
  • 10:30 - 11:00 We meet - Who are you? in 1 minute
  • 11:00 - 14:00 We will discuss and propose in OpenSpace format about:
    • Internationalization
    • Talent
    • How to set up an agency/company.
    • Remote work
    • Collaboration
    • And we are open for proposals.
       
  • 14:00 - 15:30 Lunch break and Networking
  • 15:30 - 17:30 OpenSpace
  • 17:30 - 18:00 Retrospective and feedback

Group dynamics: In this space we will encourage to meet each other, break the ice, and create an environment conducive to networking.
 
Business Panel: Who are you? What are you looking for? Prepare your business cards because you will have a panel to present your company.

Open Space: Based on everyone's feedback, the group will divide in smaller ones to discuss specific topics.

 

How can I participate?

If you are Gold or Silver sponsor then it is already included. If not, once tickets are available at the homepage, go and buy one. Prior to the event we will send you an email asking you about your expectations so we can make the best event possible.

When?

Thursday 9th, May - Starting at 10:00h.

Where?

Santa Catalina Church, Conil de la Frontera.
 

More info

If you have any doubts, send as an email at networking [at] drupal [dot] es.
